1

Tap a text box. The bubble shows up.

A small floating mic appears over WhatsApp, Gmail, Chrome, and other supported fields. Drag it wherever you like. Your keyboard stays as it is, and password fields never get the bubble.

2

Tap the bubble. Talk.

The bubble opens into a recording pill. The mic is on only while the pill is showing. Tap × and the recording is thrown away.

3

Hit ✓.

The mic turns off right away. An open model transcribes on your phone, even in airplane mode.

4

It's in your draft.

The text lands where your cursor was. Wispr Free never presses Send. If a field won't accept it, you get a Copy button instead.
