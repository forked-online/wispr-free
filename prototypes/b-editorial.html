Where your voice goes

A very short journey.

Most dictation apps send your voice to a data center and return text. Wispr Free keeps the whole loop inside your phone, so it works in airplane mode and there's nothing to leak.

Questions

Honest answers.

Does it replace my keyboard?

No. Gboard, or whatever keyboard you use, stays. Wispr Free adds a small floating mic beside supported text fields.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To see which text field is focused and insert the text you confirm. It skips password fields and never presses Send.

Will it work in every app?

No. Some editors don't accept safe insertion. In those, you get the transcript with a Copy button, and your draft is left alone.

When can I install it?

A signed APK for testers comes first, then the Play Store after policy review. It's still being built.
